## Original art

Characters, dialogue portraits, UI icons and the app icons are generated by
`tools/generate-art.mjs` (`npm run art`) — chibi/anime placeholders drawn
programmatically for this project. They carry no third-party licence.

They are placeholders in quality, not in licence: any of them can be replaced
with drawn, purchased or AI-generated art by matching the dimensions recorded in
`public/assets/art-manifest.json`. Nothing in the game reads art any other way.

## If you replace the art

Check the licence of anything you drop in, and add it here. In particular, many
"free" packs on itch.io permit *use* in a game but forbid *redistribution* of
the asset files themselves — which is exactly what committing them to a public
repository does. CC0 and CC-BY assets are safe to commit; most storefront
"free" licences are not.

Sources whose licences permit redistribution:

- **Kenney** (<https://kenney.nl/assets>) — CC0 throughout. Large, internally
  consistent, no attribution required.
- **OpenGameArt** (<https://opengameart.org>) — filter to CC0 or CC-BY. Licence
  varies per submission, so check each one.
- **Liberated Pixel Cup / LPC** — CC-BY-SA 3.0 and/or GPL 3.0. Enormous
  consistent top-down character set with a layered generator, including
  Victorian-appropriate coats and hats. Realistic proportions rather than
  chibi, and the share-alike terms carry through, so read them before using.
